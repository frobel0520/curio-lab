# 好奇一下 Curio Lab — Brand Spec

## Direction

- School: Warm Humanist
- Primary reference: Stripe Press editorial warmth
- Supporting reference: early Mailchimp conversational microcopy
- Motion reference: Headspace-style gentle easing without childlike illustration

## Design read

- Artifact: mobile-first consumer utility mother site and interactive tools
- Audience: Traditional Chinese readers in Taiwan
- Visual language: warm editorial utility
- Mode: greenfield
- Visual variance: 6/10
- Motion intensity: 4/10
- Information density: 5/10
- Asset dependence: 2/10
- Brand fidelity: 2/10

## Tokens

- Ground: `#F5F0E6`
- Surface: `#FFF9F0`
- Ink: `#241C15`
- Secondary text: `#6E685E`
- Accent: `#B85C3D`
- Hairline: `#D8CDBB`
- Display type: `Noto Serif TC`
- Body and UI type: `Noto Sans TC`
- Spacing: 4 / 8 / 16 / 24 / 40 / 64 / 96
- Radius: 12px controls / 20px panels / pill primary actions
- Shadow: `0 12px 36px rgba(36, 28, 21, 0.10)`
- Motion: 180ms control feedback / 500ms result reveal / reduced-motion fallback
- Display scale: 34–82px across calculator, section, and home-page headings; mobile hero capped at 56px

## Brand asset

The v0 uses a deliberate typographic wordmark, `好奇一下`, instead of a fabricated icon. A standalone logo asset remains a later brand task.

## Guardrails

- No purple-pink technology gradients
- No emoji as interface icons
- No stock or mismatched illustrations
- No pure black or cold gray
- One dominant action per calculator screen
- Results may be expressive; input steps remain quiet and focused
